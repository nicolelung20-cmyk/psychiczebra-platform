# Elevated AI 30-day outreach operating plan

## Operating principle

Run a focused account-based motion: research a small, relevant set of executive teams; send personalized, permission-aware outreach; and stop or adjust based on responses. Respect platform terms, opt-outs, privacy rules, and each organization’s communication preferences. This plan is not a volume target.

## Target account definition

Prioritize organizations where a senior leader publicly signals a current workflow, operational, service, or governance priority that could plausibly fit a short AI implementation. Research only public, relevant information. Do not infer sensitive traits, scrape prohibited sources, or claim knowledge you cannot substantiate.

## Four-week rhythm

| Week | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build a researched account list and tailor a specific workflow hypothesis for each. | 15-25 accounts, named sponsors, source links, and a reason for relevance. |
| 2 | Send initial individualized outreach and log replies, objections, consent, and opt-outs. | Qualified conversations and clear next steps. |
| 3 | Conduct discovery, score prospects, and send scope summaries only where fit is evidenced. | Scorecards and a small number of high-integrity proposals. |
| 4 | Follow up on open conversations, disqualify stalled or unsuitable opportunities, and review funnel assumptions. | Updated pipeline, lessons, and next-month account list. |

## Personalization checklist

Before sending, verify that the message names a public, relevant trigger; offers a bounded hypothesis rather than a promise; uses the recipient’s correct name and role; and contains a simple way to decline future contact. Do not use unverifiable “I noticed” claims, fake mutual connections, or fabricated social proof.

## Initial cold-email template

**Subject:** A bounded AI workflow question for [Company]

Hi [First name],

[Public, accurate observation about a current company priority or the recipient’s stated responsibility.] It made me wonder whether [specific workflow] is a priority for your team.

Elevated AI, from Elevated Associates LLC, helps executive teams assess and implement a narrowly defined AI workflow in a 2-6 week engagement. A typical project is $5,000-$15,000 and focuses on a working process, governance guardrails, and measures to validate—not guaranteed ROI or an unbounded transformation.

Would a 20-minute conversation to compare the workflow against this scope be useful? If not, I will not follow up.

[Name]

## LinkedIn connection note template

Hi [First name]—[accurate, public relevance]. I work with Elevated AI on short, bounded AI workflow implementations for executive teams. I’d value connecting; no automated sequence or expectation attached.

## LinkedIn message after acceptance

Thanks for connecting, [First name]. [One sentence tying a public priority to a specific workflow hypothesis.] We offer $5,000-$15,000, fixed-scope engagements to assess or implement a focused workflow with governance and measurement. Is a brief fit conversation worthwhile, or should I close the loop?

## Follow-up cadence

Use a maximum of two respectful follow-ups after the initial message unless the recipient engages, asks for material, or gives permission for another date. Stop immediately for an opt-out, a no, or signs that contact is unwelcome.

| Touch | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Initial | Day 1 | State accurate relevance and ask for a low-pressure fit conversation. |
| Follow-up 1 | 5-7 business days later | Add one useful, specific question or clarify the bounded scope. |
| Follow-up 2 | 7-10 business days later | Close the loop and provide an easy decline; do not send further outreach without new permission or a genuinely relevant future trigger. |

## Transparent pipeline math

The $50,000 goal is a planning target, not a forecast or promise. At a $10,000 average package value, it requires **five closed packages**. The conversion assumptions below show the activity and fit conditions that would be needed:

| Stage | Illustrative conversion to next stage | Needed volume for five closed packages |
|---|---:|---:|
| Closed packages | — | 5 |
| Signed proposals | 50% close rate | 10 |
| Qualified discoveries | 50% proposal rate | 20 |
| Positive replies | 40% become qualified discoveries | 50 |
| Personalized initial outreaches | 10% positive reply rate | 500 |

These figures are deliberately simple and may be wrong for this market. Review them weekly against actual response, qualification, proposal, and close rates. If the required outreach cannot be conducted compliantly and personally, reduce the target or extend the timeframe—do not raise message volume indiscriminately.
