# Elevated AI discovery-call guide

## Call objective

Determine whether a bounded $5,000-$15,000 implementation can help the prospect validate and improve one executive-priority workflow. A qualified call ends with a clear next step; an unqualified call ends respectfully with a useful reason not to proceed.

## Suggested 30-minute flow

1. **Set context (3 minutes):** “I’d like to understand the workflow and constraints before deciding whether an implementation package is useful. If there is not a clear fit, I’ll say so.”
2. **Explore the problem (12 minutes):** Ask the questions below; listen for ownership, urgency, access, and risk constraints.
3. **Reflect the hypothesis (7 minutes):** Summarize the workflow, desired change, assumptions, and what would need validation. Avoid claiming a result.
4. **Qualify and align (5 minutes):** Confirm sponsor, stakeholders, procurement/security path, timing, and investment range.
5. **Agree next step (3 minutes):** Share a written scope or disqualify. Never pressure a prospect to manufacture urgency.

## Discovery questions

**Business priority**

- Which executive objective makes this workflow worth improving now?
- What happens today, who performs it, and where does the process stall or create risk?
- What would a better process look like in observable terms? What is the current baseline?
- What decision will this work inform: pilot, production investment, operating change, or something else?

**Implementation fit**

- What is the smallest workflow that would create a meaningful learning opportunity in 2-6 weeks?
- Who owns the process, who will use it, and who can make decisions during the engagement?
- Which systems, data, or documents are involved? What access can be safely provided?
- What security, privacy, legal, procurement, or labor considerations apply?

**Commercial fit**

- Is there an executive sponsor accountable for the outcome and available for key decisions?
- Is a $5,000-$15,000 fixed-scope engagement within the planning range if the scope is appropriate?
- What is the decision process, decision date, and any prerequisite review?

## Qualification close

“Based on what you shared, the sensible next step is [a written readiness-sprint scope / a priority-workflow pilot scope / no proposal yet]. The proposed work would test [hypothesis] using [measure], with [assumptions and dependencies]. It would not guarantee [business outcome]. Does that accurately reflect the decision you need to make?”

## Disqualify or defer when

- There is no defined business workflow, executive sponsor, or accountable owner.
- The prospect needs a guaranteed ROI, fully autonomous decision-making, or a claim that AI is risk-free.
- Access to appropriate people, safe test data, or a decision path is unavailable in the package timeframe.
- Required security, legal, regulatory, or integration work clearly exceeds the proposed scope.
- The budget, timing, or scope is materially misaligned and cannot be reset transparently.

Suggested response: “I do not think this package is responsible or useful under the current constraints. The prerequisite appears to be [specific prerequisite]. We can revisit after that is in place.”
