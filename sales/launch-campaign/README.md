# Elevated AI opt-in launch campaign

This send-ready campaign is for an existing newsletter or waitlist audience that has explicitly opted in to receive relevant communications from Elevated Associates LLC. It is intended for senior B2B leaders evaluating practical AI implementation work.

Before publishing or sending, replace every `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDER]`, including the sending identity, legal address, preference-center and unsubscribe URLs, and UTM values. ## Claims and channel guardrails

- Describe the offer as AI implementation support for workflow prioritization, governance, operating model, adoption, and measurable acceptance criteria. Do not promise outcomes.
- Use only approved, accurate product details. Do not add testimonials, client logos, quantified outcomes, or urgency language without substantiation and permission.
- Keep the executive discovery call CTA exactly linked to `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/#discovery` until the production domain is confirmed and substituted.
- Honor unsubscribes immediately, retain consent evidence, and follow applicable privacy, marketing, and platform requirements.
