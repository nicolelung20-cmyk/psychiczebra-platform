# Weekly LinkedIn outreach

A small, manual, weekly routine for starting conversations with leaders who fit Elevated AI. It follows the [30-day outreach plan](30-day-outreach-plan.md): a few researched, personal notes each week, never mass connection requests or automated messaging.

## What arrives each Monday

A scheduled Claude routine ("Elevated AI weekly LinkedIn prospects (free)") runs every Monday at 13:00 UTC and sends a push notification and an email containing:

- **5 prospects:** CEOs, COOs, founders, and presidents at US companies with 51-500 employees whose companies are showing research intent for AI strategy, AI automation, or workflow automation. Each week covers a different group of states so names don't repeat.
- **A personal connection note for each prospect**, under 300 characters, tied to that company's business.
- **Two LinkedIn post drafts** for Nicole's profile or the company page.

The routine uses only Vibe Prospecting's free preview. It never exports, enriches, or buys credits, and it never sends anything.

## Weekly checklist (about 20 minutes)

1. Open each prospect's LinkedIn profile, check that the person and company still match, then choose **Connect**, then **Add a note**, and paste the note. Edit anything that doesn't sound like you.
2. Publish one or both post drafts, or skip them if they don't fit the week.
3. Record each note sent in the [campaign tracking sheet](launch-campaign/campaign-tracking-sheet.md): name, company, date, and status.
4. When someone accepts, send the follow-up below **once**. If they don't reply, stop.
5. If someone replies with interest, run the [discovery-call guide](discovery-call-guide.md) and score them with the [qualification scorecard](prospect-qualification-scorecard.md).

## Connection note pattern

> Hi [First name], [one specific, public observation about their company or role]. I help leadership teams get one workflow running on AI in a few weeks, fixed scope. Would be glad to connect. - Nicole, Elevated AI

Rules: name something real and public, suggest one plausible workflow as a question rather than a claim, and never promise results, revenue, or savings.

## Follow-up after they accept (send once)

> Thanks for connecting. If one slow or repetitive workflow is on your mind, I offer a free 30-minute fit call to see whether AI can help: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/#discovery. No worries if the timing isn't right.

## Guardrails

- Keep it to about 5 to 10 new notes per week, all sent by hand. No connection or messaging automation tools, which break LinkedIn's terms.
- Don't copy prospect lists into this repository. Keep names in the tracking sheet only.
- Honor every "not interested" immediately, and don't contact that person again.
