# Elevated AI executive implementation package

**Provider:** Elevated Associates LLC
**Product:** Elevated AI
**Ideal buyer:** An executive sponsor with a defined, high-value workflow to improve and a team that can participate in a short implementation.

## The practical outcome

Elevated AI helps a leadership team turn one priority workflow into an adoption-ready AI operating pattern: a clearly bounded use case, a working implementation or prototype, governance guardrails, and a measurement plan.

We frame value as a hypothesis to validate with the client. We do not promise revenue, cost savings, model accuracy, employee adoption, regulatory approval, or a particular business result.

## Delivery scope and responsibilities

**Elevated AI provides**

- A kickoff and structured working sessions with the named sponsor and subject-matter experts.
- Workflow mapping, use-case prioritization, and documented success measures.
- Configuration or prototype work limited to the selected package and written acceptance criteria.
- Enablement materials, a handoff session, and risks/assumptions documented in the final readout.

**The client provides**

- One accountable executive sponsor and timely access to relevant workflow owners.
- Approved sample data or a safe test environment; no sensitive data is requested unless separately approved.
- Decisions on priorities, legal/security requirements, and acceptance criteria.

## Boundaries that protect both sides

The engagement is not a substitute for legal, security, privacy, HR, financial, or regulatory advice. Elevated AI does not represent that AI output is always accurate or appropriate without human review. Production deployment, broad change management, integrations beyond the stated scope, and outcome guarantees require a separate agreement.

## Ethical claim guidelines

- State what was delivered, observed, or measured; do not convert a hypothesis into a fact.
- Use client names, logos, quotes, metrics, and case studies only with written permission.
- Attribute quantified results to the specific client, period, baseline, and measurement method.
- Do not imply endorsement, partnership, certification, or regulatory compliance without proof and approval.
- Describe limitations, required human oversight, and material assumptions when they affect a buying decision.
- Never invent urgency, social proof, customer pain, or meeting context. Personalization must be public, accurate, and relevant.
