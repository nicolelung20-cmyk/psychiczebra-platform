# Elevated AI prospect qualification scorecard

Use this scorecard after discovery. Record evidence rather than assumptions. A score is a decision aid, not a prediction of deal close or client outcomes.

| Criterion | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive priority | No business priority identified | General interest in AI | Named executive objective and workflow |
| Sponsor and owner | Neither named | One contact but unclear authority | Sponsor and workflow owner committed |
| Bounded use case | Broad transformation request | Candidate workflow is vague | One achievable workflow is defined |
| Access and participation | No access path | Partial or uncertain access | SMEs, safe data, and decision time available |
| Risk readiness | Constraints unknown | Constraints identified but unresolved | Security/privacy/legal path is understood |
| Measurement | No observable success condition | Desired result only | Baseline and acceptance measure can be set |
| Commercial fit | No budget or timing | One is tentative | $5k-$15k range and decision window align |

**Total:** `/14`

| Result | Guidance |
|---:|---|
| 11-14 | Strong fit: prepare a fixed-scope proposal with dependencies and acceptance criteria. |
| 8-10 | Conditional fit: close the named gaps before proposal or offer a readiness sprint. |
| 0-7 | Disqualify or defer: share the prerequisite, not artificial urgency. |

## Required disqualification check

Do not propose an implementation package if the prospect expects guaranteed outcomes, requires handling data that cannot be safely approved for the engagement, lacks an accountable sponsor, or has requirements plainly outside the delivery scope. Note the reason and any ethical re-entry condition in the CRM.
